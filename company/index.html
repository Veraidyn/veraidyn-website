About

What we are doing.

AI agents are already acting inside real systems. They read data, call tools, and make decisions that used to need a person. The controls around them were built for software that behaves the same way every time.

Veraidyn measures whether an agent can be trusted with what it is about to do, continuously, and enforces that judgment at runtime. It runs on top of the stack you already have.

We model trust the way it works between people, because every model reasons through language. That makes the method independent of whichever model ships next.
